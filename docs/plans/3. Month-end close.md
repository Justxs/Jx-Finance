# Plan: Month-end close

Status: planned 2026-09-25. Size M. Build this last of the three plans. Its checklist counts unusual rows from [Unusual amounts](<2. Unusual amounts.md>), and its reminder travels through the publisher from [Discord notifications](<1. Discord notifications.md>).

## Outcome

A page per calendar month walks the bookkeeping session to its end. It has three parts:

1. **What still needs attention:** uncategorised rows, unconfirmed recurring entries and unusual amounts, each with a link to the rows.
2. **What the month looked like:** income, expense, net and savings rate against the previous month; the categories that moved most; budgets against actual; and the net worth change.
3. **A Close button with an optional note.** Closing freezes a snapshot of the month's figures. Nothing is locked. If anything dated in that month changes later, the page and the edit dialog say so, show the difference and list the rows behind it, and the month can be re-closed to accept the change or reopened.

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision | Rejected | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strictness | Soft close: a snapshot plus drift detection; every write path stays as it is | A hard lock refusing writes dated in a closed month | A lock would need a check in `SaveChangesAsync` and in about eight `ExecuteUpdate`/`ExecuteDelete` sites. It would also need exceptions for deleting a category or tag and for a reporting-currency change, all of which legitimately rewrite old months. A snapshot gives the traceability ("what changed after I closed August") without refusing a correction |
| Whose close | Per user, keyed by the month and by the scope it was taken under: the active household id, or none for "Everything" | One close per household | Figures depend on the active household filter. A per-household close would be one member's snapshot of a view another member sees differently. Budgets and net worth snapshots, which the page uses, are per user already |
| Which months | Only a month that has ended in the installation time zone | Closing the current month early | A snapshot of an unfinished month would drift by design |
| Figures source | The snapshot is the report summary of the month exactly as `ReportService.GetSummaryAsync` answers it, stored as jsonb | A separate calculation | One definition of income and expense. Drift is a plain comparison of two answers of the same function |
| Comparison | A new `ReportComparisonMode.PreviousMonth`: the same calendar span one month earlier, with a range ending on a month end ending on a month end again | Reusing `PreviousPeriod` | `PreviousPeriod` counts days, so March would meet 29 January to 28 February. The new mode also helps the reports page |
| Budgets | Monthly budgets only, with usage computed as of the month's last day; the page says limits are today's limits | Weekly, quarterly and yearly windows; stored budget history | Budgets keep no history, and a window that straddles the month cannot be split honestly |
| Net worth | The last snapshot on or before the last day of the previous month, against the last one on or before this month's last day. Gaps are named ("from 29 Jul") | Recomputing past values | Assets and debts keep only their current value; snapshots are the only history |

## Data model

| Change | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| New `MonthClose` (`OwnableEntity`) | `Month` (`DateOnly`, first day), `HouseholdId?` (the scope), `ClosedAt`, `Note?` (max 1000), `Snapshot` (jsonb complex type: reporting currency, total income, total expense, net, transaction count, the category and synthetic-group breakdown for income and expense, the net worth at month end when known). Unique filtered index on `(UserId, Month, HouseholdId)` with nulls not distinct. Not soft-deleted: reopening deletes the row. Not in the trash. Not audited, because it is personal |
| `Feature.MonthClose` | `HasDefaultValue(true)` |
| `NotificationType.MonthReadyToClose` | Appended. Payload gains `Month?` |

## Backend steps

1. **Comparison mode.** Add `PreviousMonth` to `ReportComparisonMode` and `ComparisonWindow.For`, with unit tests: 31 March against February, February against January in a leap year, and a mid-month range.
2. **Budgets as of a date.** Add `IBudgetUsageCalculator.CalculateAsync(budgets, DateOnly asOf, ct)`. The existing overload passes `clock.Today`. `Walk()` takes the date instead of reading the clock. Test that a past month's usage and rollover match what the page showed on that day.
3. **Uncategorised filter.** `GetTransactionsRequest.Uncategorized` (bool) keeps rows with no category, or with a split line without one. The ledger has lacked it, and the checklist needs its link.
4. **Service.** `Endpoints/MonthClose/Services/MonthCloseService` has three entry points:
   - `GetMonthAsync(month)` returns the status (`open`, `closed`, `closedChanged`, `notEnded`), the note and `closedAt`. It also returns:
     - **checklist counts:** uncategorised rows in the month; active recurring entries whose `NextDueDate` is on or before the month's last day; unusual rows not dismissed, when that feature is on; imports for the month's statement, as the date of the latest imported row per account;
     - **figures:** the current report summary of the month with `PreviousMonth`;
     - monthly budget usage as of the last day;
     - the two net worth points;
     - **drift**, when closed: the snapshot figures, the current ones, their differences per total and per category, and the changed rows. Changed rows are transactions and investment entries dated in the month with `UpdatedAt > ClosedAt`, soft-deleted ones included through `IgnoreQueryFilters(QueryFilters.SoftDelete)`. There are at most 100, each marked created, edited or deleted.
     Rows edited later but reverted leave no figure change. They are still listed, labelled "edited, figures unchanged".
   - `CloseAsync(month, note)` is refused with `monthClose.notEnded` for an unfinished month. It writes or replaces the snapshot for the current scope. Re-closing is how drift is accepted.
   - `ReopenAsync(month)` deletes the row, and a repeat is a no-op. `UpdateNoteAsync(month, note)` edits the note.
   - A reporting-currency change since the close is reported as `currencyChanged`, with no per-figure drift. Every figure moves then, and the difference would mean nothing.
5. **Check the timestamps.** Every set-based update that rewrites transactions must stamp `UpdatedAt`, or drift misses it:
   - `CategorizationRuleService.RunAsync` already does.
   - `CategoryService.DeleteAsync`'s clearing of `CategoryId`, `TrashService`'s category restore and `SettingsService`'s revaluation must be checked and fixed where they do not.
   - `BulkTagAsync` and tag delete change only `TransactionTags`. Tags are not in the month figures, so they are deliberately not drift. The feature doc says so.
   - An integration test per site asserts the stamp.
6. **Endpoints** (`MonthCloseGroup`, `ApiTags.MonthClose`, gated by the feature):
   - `GET /api/month-close?year=2026` returns twelve statuses for the month picker, in one query.
   - `GET /api/month-close/{yyyy-MM}` returns the review above.
   - `POST /api/month-close/{yyyy-MM}` closes or re-closes, with body `{ note? }`.
   - `PUT /api/month-close/{yyyy-MM}/note` updates the note.
   - `DELETE /api/month-close/{yyyy-MM}` reopens.
   - Invalid months answer `monthClose.invalidMonth`. `monthClose.notEnded` maps to 409.
7. **Reminder.** `MonthCloseReminderJob : PeriodicJob` runs daily, gated by the feature. On days 1 to 5 of a month, it raises one `MonthReadyToClose` through `INotificationPublisher` for each active user who has closed at least one month and has not closed the previous one. Having closed a month once is the opt-in. It is deduplicated per user and month.

## Frontend steps

1. `just gen`. Add the three mutations to `invalidation.ts`. They invalidate the month-close root. Transaction, transfer, conversion and investment mutations also invalidate the month-close root, so drift appears without a reload. Add `serverErrors.monthClose.*` in both locales.
2. **Route.** `routes/close.tsx` with search `{ month?: "yyyy-MM" }`, defaulting to the latest ended month that is not closed; `requireFeature("monthClose")`; and a loader that warms the year list and the month review.
   - **Sidebar:** add an entry to `navPages` in the `review` group, with shortcut `m`, which is free.
   - **Command palette:** "Close last month" as an action entry.
3. **Page.** `features/month-close/` with these components:
   - `month-picker`: previous and next, and a strip of twelve months with open, closed and changed markers carried by icon and text;
   - `close-checklist`: count rows, each linking to the ledger with `dateFrom`/`dateTo` plus `uncategorized`, `unusual` or the recurring page;
   - `month-figures`: reuses `report-stats`, `change-badge` and `breakdown-list`, with the five largest category movers;
   - `month-budgets`;
   - `month-net-worth`;
   - `drift-panel`: difference per total and category, and the changed rows with links;
   - `close-form`: note and Close, Re-close or Reopen, with a confirm when the checklist is not empty.
   Each component has stories for open, closed, closed-changed, currency changed, not ended, empty month, pending and `failWith`.
4. **Edit dialog hint.** When a transaction, transfer or conversion being edited or created is dated in a month the user closed under the current scope, the dialog shows "August is closed. Saving will show as a change after close." The closed months come from the year query, which is already cached by the route or fetched on demand. This is a hint only; saving is never blocked.
5. **Reports.** Add "Previous month" to the comparison select on the reports page.
6. **Bell.** The `MonthReadyToClose` producer links to `/close?month=…`, with texts in en/lt. `NotificationTexts` on the server gets the same text, for Discord.
7. **Dashboard, optional.** A `MonthClose` card appended to `DashboardCard`, showing "August is ready to close" with the checklist counts. Leave it out of v1 if the page is enough.

## Tests

- **Unit:** the `PreviousMonth` window; `BudgetWindow` walking from a past `asOf`; month parsing.
- **Integration:**
  - Closing an ended month stores the report's figures, and closing the current month is refused.
  - Editing, adding and deleting a row dated in the month produce drift with the right row list and differences.
  - Editing a row dated in another month produces none.
  - Moving a row out of the month by its date shows as drift in the old month.
  - Re-close clears the drift, and reopen removes the close.
  - Closes are separate per active household scope and per user, and a household partner's edit on a shared account shows as drift for the owner who closed.
  - A category delete's rewrite is stamped and shows as drift.
  - A reporting-currency change gives `currencyChanged`.
  - The checklist counts match the ledger filter they link to.
  - The reminder is raised only for users who closed before, once per month.
  - Switching the feature off gives 404 `feature.disabled`.
  - A backup round-trip keeps the closes.

## Later, not in this plan

- An optional hard lock on top of this, if drift turns out to be ignored in practice. The snapshot and the changed-rows query would stay; only the refusal would be new.
- A close that also emails or posts the month summary. That is the monthly summary in the backlog, and a closed month is the natural moment to send it.
