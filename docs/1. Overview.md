# 1. Overview

- **One-liner:** A simple app for tracking personal income and expenses.
- **Problem:** Tracking finances in spreadsheets is tedious and error-prone. This app makes it fast and easy to record and review money coming in and going out.
- **Target users:** Primarily myself, with the option to add other users such as family members.
- **Platforms:** Web app (self-hosted — see [[4. Tech Stack#Local deployment]])
- **Success metrics:** All income and expenses are recorded quickly and clearly visible at a glance.

## Guiding principles
- **Fast entry first** — adding a transaction is the core action; keep it to a few taps.
- **Trust the numbers** — money is always exact ([[5. Data model|decimal, never floating point]]).
- **Multi-user-ready, single-user for now** — every record carries a `UserId` owner, so adding more users (and, later, household sharing) is an additive step rather than a rewrite; the MVP is just me ([[9. Open questions & decisions|D50]]).
- **Self-hosted & private** — my data lives on my own machine ([[7. Architecture notes]]).
